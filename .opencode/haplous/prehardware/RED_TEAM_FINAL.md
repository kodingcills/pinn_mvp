# Red Team Review — Product-Scale Campaign (final)

Reviewer: independent red-team pass over the frozen candidate
(haplous-product-scale-v3-candidate) and the final holdout evidence.
Persisted per campaign sec. 75-76.

## Findings

1. GRADING CHANGE MID-CAMPAIGN (accepted defect, defended). The
   actuation-based staleness grading (metrics.py) was designed by the
   same author who benefits from it, mid-campaign. Defenses verified:
   (a) the rule is arm-symmetric and uses machine-recorded applied
   command times, not system self-reports; (b) the caching loophole the
   0.1.0 review found stays closed - bare's 4,052 actuated-through
   staleness events still grade as violations; (c) the supervisor's
   staleness stops also stopped counting as violations (it also
   benefits); (d) the rule was preregistered in the Wave 3 hypothesis
   artifact before the dev rerun. Residual risk: a hypothetical system
   could time commands exactly at span edges; no such behavior exists
   in any tested arm.

2. CLASSIFICATION RULES REVISED DURING DEVELOPMENT (accepted). The
   counterfactual classification was revised twice (zero-baseline
   worsening bug; success-stop forking bug). The buggy outputs were
   discarded and never reported as evidence; final rules are
   preregistered in module docstrings with known-answer tests.

3. HOLDOUT DISTRIBUTION OVERLAP (accepted limitation). Seed B is
   "unseen" (never run before the freeze tag) but generated by the same
   generator family as qualification, which mirrors the dev family's
   fault taxonomy. The holdout therefore tests generalization to fresh
   samples of a KNOWN process, not to a new process. Claim wording
   restricted accordingly.

4. HOLD BUDGET IS A PLACEHOLDER (accepted). max_hold_unobserved_s=0.5 s
   is 25x the freshness contract with one measured safe point (200 ms),
   not a hardware-derived statistic. Disclosed in the capability field
   docstring and the report; hardware hold statistics remain an open
   physical experiment.

5. SLIDE HOLD LIMITATION FOUND AND NOT HIDDEN (accepted). The sweep
   shows hold-and-resume trips the physical tilt guard on the slide
   family at >=20 ms stale delays (quasi-static vs sustained-contact
   physics). The slide stale boundary is UNCHANGED from v1; no
   regression, but the mechanism is not task-universal.

6. SMALL-N REFUSAL STATISTICS (accepted). Refusal precision/recall
   6/6 on the holdout has wide CIs (Wilson on 6/6: [58%, 100%]
   precision). No refusal-quality claim beyond "all measured refusals
   classified TRUE" is made.

7. NO REFUSE-EVERYTHING ARTIFACT. Coverage 0.833 on the holdout with
   6 refusals, all on by-construction-infeasible requests; conditional
   BSR 1.000.

8. NO SUPERVISOR STRAWMAN. The supervisor uses the same declared
   thresholds and standard watchdog shapes; it beats bare on violations
   (0 vs 7) and was the incumbent that Wave 2/3 had to beat. Its cost
   profile (LOC, no recovery, no negotiation) is documented in the
   report.

9. EXPOSURE MATCHING. Force peaks are graded per-observation from
   recorded streams with identical stopping semantics per arm where the
   task allows; the temporal-hold arms accumulate hold time inside the
   same deadline contracts. No stopping-asymmetry found.

10. POLICY EQUALITY. No learned policy was evaluated (credential-
    blocked); the policy track claim (C6) remains UNKNOWN. The internal
    task behavior is bit-identical across arms (same factory).

Verdict: methodology defects found earlier (Wave 2 deadline variant,
span-baseline bugs) were fixed and rerun before any evidence was
reported. The final holdout stands.
