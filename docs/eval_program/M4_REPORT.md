## 11. What this does NOT show

- SCRIPTED-POLICY SIMULATION: every arm drives the same scripted MuJoCo behaviour. Nothing here measures a VLA or any learned policy.
- No OEM or hardware claim. The machine, its faults and its physics are simulated.
- Adapter machines (G1 asymmetry 1) are untested: no behaviour authorizes a command-mode substitute, so G1 and Haplous both refuse there by construction.
- G1 is mildly strong: it reads the machine's qualification record (`expected_duration_s`, `max_initial_offset_m`) for free and has authorized budgeted retry. The disclosed asymmetries (G1 design section 6) favour G1, which is conservative for Haplous claims.
- Slides with translational disturbances and the two named slide scenarios are excluded as mesh artifacts (16-tile annulus facet taps, KNOWN_ISSUES); slide results cover only undisturbed or rotationally disturbed slides.
- `docs/HES_V0_REPORT.md` is stale on main (digest drift, KNOWN_ISSUES) and is not M4 evidence; epoch `hes-m4-e2` is not poolable with it or any earlier epoch.
- HES intervention-quality metrics need counterfactual forks, not run here (NOT_AVAILABLE).
- Agreement between Haplous and G1 is partly by construction: hold budget, recovery episode and restart from the live pose use the same machine and behaviour hooks (G1 design section 6, item 7). A tie here is not evidence that either guard set is sufficient, only that they coincide on these populations.
- The stress population probes the regions named by the M4.1 verifier; its rates are not field rates.
